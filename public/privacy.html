Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

1. Who we are

DiagnosticOS is operated by [COMPANY NAME] ([REGISTRATION NUMBER]), [ADDRESS], South Africa ("we"). Our Information Officer is [NAME], reachable at [EMAIL].

2. Our role under POPIA

For the personal information of a workshop's own customers (names, phone numbers, emails, vehicles, service history), the workshop is the responsible party and we are its operator: we process that information only on the workshop's instructions to provide the service. For the workshop's own account holders and team members, we are the responsible party.

3. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, password (stored only as a salted hash), workshop details, plan and billing status.
  • Workshop records entered by the workshop: customers, vehicles, invoices, inspections, remaps, jobs, parts, scan results and fault codes.
  • Technical data: IP address and request logs for security and fault-finding, and an audit trail of actions taken in the account (we do not store the content of requests in the audit trail).

4. Why we use it

To provide and secure the service, process subscription payments, send service emails (verification, password reset, invoices the workshop asks us to send), and, where the workshop has switched it on, send service reminders to its customers. We do not sell personal information.

5. Sharing

We share information only with providers needed to run the service: hosting [HOSTING PROVIDER, REGION], payment processing (PayFast), email delivery ([EMAIL PROVIDER]) and, if enabled, SMS/WhatsApp delivery ([SMS PROVIDER]). Information may be processed outside South Africa by these providers; we use them only where they provide adequate protection as required by POPIA section 72.

6. Retention

We keep workshop data while the account is active. When a workshop deletes its account, all of its data is permanently deleted from the live system; backups are overwritten within [30] days.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, and object to processing. Workshop admins can download all account data and permanently delete the account from Settings. A workshop's customers should contact the workshop first; we will help the workshop respond. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (inforeg.org.za).

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS); passwords are hashed; each workshop's data is isolated from every other workshop's; access is logged. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected parties and the Information Regulator of a breach as POPIA requires.
